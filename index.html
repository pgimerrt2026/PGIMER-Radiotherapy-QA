Monthly Absolute Output Verification — Photon Beams

TrueBeam Linear Accelerator — SVC Site · Absolute dosimetry per IAEA TRS-398 formalism

%
Reference conditions: T₀ = 20 °C, P₀ = 1013.25 mbar
Background & Formalism — Record Reference

Purpose

Monthly constancy check of the absolute photon dose output of the accelerator against the calibrated baseline established at commissioning, expressed in cGy/MU, per AERB QA schedule and AAPM TG-142.

Formalism (IAEA TRS-398, Section 6.4)

Absorbed dose to water at the reference depth: Dw,Q = MQ · ND,w,Qo · kQ,Qo, where MQ is the fully corrected chamber reading (temperature–pressure, polarity and ion-recombination corrected), ND,w,Qo is the chamber's calibration factor in terms of absorbed dose to water at the reference quality Qo (subscript Qo omitted here as calibration is referenced to 60Co), and kQ,Qo is the beam-quality correction factor for the actual beam quality Q, obtained from the TPR20,10 tab or the chamber's calibration certificate.

Correction factors used

kpol corrects for polarity effect: kpol = (|M+| + |M−|) / (2M), M being the reading at the routine (normal, V1) polarity. ks (ion recombination / saturation correction, two-voltage method, TRS-398 Eq. 14) corrects for incomplete charge collection: ks = a0 + a1(M1/M2) + a2(M1/M2)², with a0,a1,a2 taken from TRS-398 Table 9 at the polarizing-voltage ratio V1/V2 entered in the setup panel — any voltage pair may be typed (e.g. 300/150, 400/100); a ratio between the table's grid points (2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 5.0) is linearly interpolated, and a ratio outside 2.0–5.0 is flagged and clamped to the nearest tabulated value. kTP corrects chamber response to reference temperature/pressure (T0 = 20 °C, P0 = 1013.25 mbar).

Tolerance

±2% of baseline output (AERB Safety Code / AAPM TG-142 monthly action level), unless a tighter departmental tolerance is specified.

Background & Formalism — Record Reference

Purpose

Monthly constancy check of the absolute electron dose output of the accelerator against the calibrated baseline established at commissioning, expressed in cGy/MU, per AERB QA schedule and AAPM TG-142.

Formalism (IAEA TRS-398, Section 7.4)

Absorbed dose to water: Dw,Q = MQ · ND,w,Qo · kQ,Qo, with the chamber reference point positioned at the reference depth zref in water. TRS-398 specifies the electron beam-quality index as R50 (half-value depth, g/cm²), with zref = 0.6·R50 − 0.1 g/cm². Where departmental protocol measures at a fixed reference depth and normalises via a percentage depth-dose factor instead, that is recorded in the "PDD/TPR (%)" field per energy.

kpol corrects for polarity effect: kpol = (|M+| + |M−|) / (2M), M being the reading at the routine (normal, V1) polarity. ks (ion recombination / saturation correction, two-voltage method, TRS-398 Eq. 14) corrects for incomplete charge collection: ks = a0 + a1(M1/M2) + a2(M1/M2)², with a0,a1,a2 taken from TRS-398 Table 9 at the polarizing-voltage ratio V1/V2 entered in the setup panel — any voltage pair may be typed (e.g. 300/150, 400/100); a ratio between the table's grid points (2.0, 2.5, 3.0, 3.5, 4.0, 5.0) is linearly interpolated, and a ratio outside 2.0–5.0 is flagged and clamped to the nearest tabulated value. kTP corrects chamber response to reference temperature/pressure (T0 = 20 °C, P0 = 1013.25 mbar).

±2% of baseline output (AERB Safety Code / AAPM TG-142 monthly action level), unless a tighter departmental tolerance is specified.

Photon Beam Quality Verification — TPR20,10

TrueBeam Linear Accelerator — SVC Site · Beam-quality index per IAEA TRS-398, Table 12 / Section 6.3

%
SCD = 100 cm · Field 10×10 cm² at SCD
Background & Formalism — Record Reference

Definition (IAEA TRS-398, Table 12 / Section 6.3)

TPR20,10 — tissue–phantom ratio in water at depths of 20 and 10 g/cm², for a 10 cm × 10 cm field defined at the plane of the detector with a source–chamber distance (SCD) of 100 cm — the beam-quality index recommended by TRS-398 for high-energy photon beams, used in place of the accelerator potential or PDD(10) because it is largely independent of electron contamination of the incident beam.

TPR20,10 = M20 / M10, the ratio of the (ion-recombination and polarity corrected) chamber readings at 20 and 10 g/cm² depth, SCD fixed at 100 cm for both readings so the field size at the chamber is constant. Per TRS-398 Table 12, recombination effects at the two depths should be checked and corrected for if they differ appreciably with depth.

Use of TPR20,10

TRS-398 Table 13 specifies the reference depth for absolute dosimetry: zref = 10 g/cm² for TPR20,10 ≥ 0.70, and 10 g/cm² (or 5 g/cm²) for TPR20,10 < 0.70. The measured TPR20,10 also indexes the chamber-specific beam-quality correction factor kQ,Qo via TRS-398 Table 14 (adapted from Andreo) — this sheet linearly interpolates that table for the selected cylindrical chamber, for record and cross-check purposes. The resulting kQ,Qo is a reference estimate only — verify against the chamber's own calibration certificate before use in the Photon Output tab.

Monitoring purpose

Beyond its role in absolute calibration, tracking TPR20,10 month to month is a sensitive check of accelerator beam-energy (spectral) stability, independent of the absolute output check.

15. Dosimetric Leaf Gap (DLG) / Leaf Separation AB

Sliding-gap sweep method (LoSasso et al. 1998): average MLC transmission RT measured, then for each nominal gap g the corrected reading is Rg′ = Rg − RT·(1−g/W), where W is the sliding-window travel length. A linear fit g(Rg′) = a·Rg′ + b is made; DLG is the extrapolated gap at Rg′ = 0, i.e. |b|. No pass/fail tolerance is specified in the source records — DLG is a reported, trended value, checked against the vendor-provided reference.
